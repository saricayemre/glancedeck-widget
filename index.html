GlanceDeck Widget – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Developer / Geliştirici: SARICAYE · Contact / İletişim: y.emre.sarica@hotmail.com

This policy applies to the GlanceDeck Widget app for Windows, published by SARICAYE on the Microsoft Store. / Bu politika, SARICAYE tarafından Microsoft Store'da yayınlanan GlanceDeck Widget uygulaması için geçerlidir.

English

GlanceDeck Widget ("the app") does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

  • No account: no sign-in or personal information is required.
  • Local data: settings, notes, calendar events, reminders and alarms are stored only on your computer and are never uploaded.
  • Weather: when you choose a city, its name (for search) and coordinates (for the forecast) are sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). These requests do not identify you.
  • Internet radio: the station list comes from radio-browser.info; the selected stream is played directly from the station's server.
  • System information: CPU, memory, disk, network and GPU data are read locally only to display them.
  • Keyboard: if the optional Win+W shortcut is enabled, only that key combination is detected; no other keystrokes are recorded or sent.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics or telemetry.
